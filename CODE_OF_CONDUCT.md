# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want FreePlots to be a place where anyone can learn, ask and contribute, regardless of experience, background, identity or language. We pledge to make participation harassment-free and welcoming.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific: critique the code, the data or the argument, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before rewriting someone else's work.
- Keep discussions on topic and move long side conversations elsewhere.
- Credit sources, data sets and prior work.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change that does not fit the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory jokes or sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading results, hidden data or fabricated citations.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews or discussions.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat and any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the repository's security/contact channel. Reports are read in confidence; the maintainers will explain the next step and avoid conflicts of interest. Consequences range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban, decided by the maintainers.
